# Herb and Apple Stuffing
Inspired by Ina Garten.

## Ingredients
- 16 cups 1-inch bread cubes, white or whole wheat (2 baguettes)
- 4 tablespoons unsalted butter (1/2 stick)
- 2 cups medium-diced yellow onion (2 large)
- 2 cups medium-diced celery (3 large stalks)
- 2 Granny Smith apples, unpeeled, cored and large diced
- 2 tablespoons chopped flat-leaf parsley
- 1 1/2 teaspoons minced fresh rosemary leaves
- 2 teaspoons kosher salt
- 1/2 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- 1 cup chicken stock (or use vegetarian chicken broth)
- 1/2 cup sliced blanched almonds, toasted, (optional

## Directions
1. Preheat oven to 300 degrees F.
2. Put the bread cubes on a 13 by 18 by 1-inch baking sheet and bake them in the oven for 7 minutes. (skip if using pre-cooked stuffing cubes)
3. In a large saute pan, melt the butter and add the onion, celery, apples, parsley, rosemary, salt, and pepper.
4. Saute for 10 minutes, until the mixture is soft.
5. Combine the bread cubes and cooked vegetables in a large bowl and add the chicken stock, and almonds.
6. Place the stuffing into the main cavity of the turkey and into the neck of the bird. Cook a 12-pound turkey for 2 1/2 hours at 350
degrees F in a preheated oven, or bake stuffing in covered casserole pan for 45min at 350 degrees.
